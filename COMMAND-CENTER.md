# Command center

The operator's view of The Warm Channel go-to-market: where the business stands, what blocks revenue, what to decide, and what to do this week. Refreshed in the weekly triage (Mondays) and rebuilt in each monthly snapshot. Every line points at the file that governs it.

**As of 2026-10-07.** Next refresh 2026-10-12.

## State of the business, in one paragraph

Pre-launch. Solo founder, no entity, no tax number, no website live, no client, no revenue, no delivered engagement, and an empty, closed proof set. The strategy is settled: a done-for-you B2B lead generation channel built on in-person events, sold to six-figure-ACV vendors whose buyers will not answer cold outreach (`reference/positioning.md`). The landing page and calculator are drafted, the design system is built, and the method is written down. What stands between today and the first paid engagement is not strategy. It is **five decisions and two pieces of plumbing**: a registered entity, a written consent position, the guest-promise conflict (D11), the promise and its remedy, and a price.
