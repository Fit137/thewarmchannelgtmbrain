# Glossary, canonical vocabulary

Every noun in every asset is checked here. Mirror the canonical form exactly, including capitals. The audience column says where a term may appear: C is client-facing, G is guest-facing, I is internal only.
last_reviewed: 2026-10-07

## The business and the category

| Term | Where | Rule |
|---|---|---|
| The Warm Channel | C, I | Always "The Warm Channel" with a capital T, mid-sentence too. Never "Warm Channel" alone, never "TWC" outside internal tables. Guest use is D12 |
| Done-for-you B2B lead generation channel built on in-person events | C, I | The canonical category. Short form: "a lead generation channel that happens to run in person" |
| In person, in-person | C, G | Adjective hyphenated before a noun, open after a verb. Not "IRL", not "live events" |
| Composed attendance | Banned | Retired category from before 2026-09-22. Never in client or guest copy |
| Convening, convenor | I | A component, not the product. Never the client headline |
| Events agency | C | Only in the negation "This isn't an events agency." We are never one |
| Format | I | Dinner, roundtable, summit, side event, suite. A delivery detail. Never in a client headline, never on the website. A guest invitation describes its own evening honestly |

## Distinctions that must never blur

| Pair | Why |
|---|---|
| 70% attendance vs 70% reply rate | The first is a cleared Clutch category benchmark. The second is the founder's unaudited figure, banned until D3. Never in the same conversation |
| Describing the work vs claiming a result | "We work every conversation afterwards" describes the service. "Our conversations convert" claims a result we do not have |
| Purpose vs promise | The channel exists to produce sales-qualified leads. Nothing promises a number of them |
| Pay-on-attendance vs our differentiator | A category norm among good agencies. Never sold as unique |

**The single distinction that matters most.** A guest and a sales-qualified lead are the same person seen from two sides. The client hears lead, pipeline and named account. The guest hears question, peers and evening. Neither side may ever read the other's word, because a guest who learns they were a lead does not come back, and returning guests are the only asset this business compounds.
