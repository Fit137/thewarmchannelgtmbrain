# Service status ledger

Governs what may be offered, described or promised. Update this file the moment a status changes, before any copy follows. Renamed from feature-status because a services business ships capabilities, not features.
last_reviewed: 2026-10-08

## Legend

- **DELIVERED**, performed for a paying client at least once. May be described as experience. Nothing is DELIVERED yet.
- **READY**, defined, and the founder can perform it now. Describe what it does in the present tense of the offer ("we plan", "we research"). Never as a result achieved ("we have filled", "our clients see").
- **IN BUILD**, committed, but the method or artifact is unfinished. Say "in development", or omit. Never shown as finished.
- **BLOCKED**, defined but cannot be sold or delivered until a named decision lands. Mention only with the dependency, never with a date.
- **ROADMAP**, vision. Never a launch claim, never implied.
- **EXCLUDED**, not offered, by design. Say so plainly when asked, and say it is deliberate.

## Offer components

- Channel strategy, which formats, moments, accounts and calendar: READY.
- Coverage read, one-segment sample on a named account list: READY. Founding-client scope and turnaround are D13.
- Coverage read, full: READY, white glove, reserved for qualified prospects.
- Audience build, identifying the named individuals at each account: READY.
- Audience build, outreach to named executives: BLOCKED, D5. Low volume, researched, one approach, no sequence once cleared.
- Confirmation and show-rate management through to the date: READY as method. Never delivered.
- Programme running, conversation design for the evening: READY.
- Facilitation on the day by the founder: READY, default attend the first three, D7. Absorbed, never billed separately.
- Post-event conversion, working conversations into sales-qualified leads: BLOCKED, D11 and D5.
- Handover to the client's sellers "with the context of what was actually said": BLOCKED, D11. Contradicts the guest's candour.
- Measurement and attribution, reported in pipeline terms: IN BUILD. No instrumentation or report format exists.
- Composition report, targeted against invited, accepted and arrived, by account: IN BUILD. Template only, no sample data.
- Weekly confirmation report against target: IN BUILD.
- Written summary of the evening for guests: READY as method.

## Supply and channels

- Demonstration room: BLOCKED, D5 and runway.
- Institutional partnership route: READY to open today.
- Founder direct approach to guests: BLOCKED, D5.
- Nomination from confirmed guests: BLOCKED until a first event happens.
- Founding guest cohort: zero of twenty. Never claimed.
- Standing guest network, invitation universe across clients: ROADMAP. Hard ban on implying it exists.
- Multi-city or multi-segment programme: ROADMAP.

## Trust and compliance

- Legal entity: NONE. Never write "Inc.", "Ltd.", a business number or a registered address.
- Written CASL and PIPEDA position: NOT HELD, D5.
- Contact record system: NOT BUILT. Designed after D5, outside this repository.
- Case studies, references, testimonials, logos: NONE.
- Founder credentials: gated, see `reference/founder-credentials.md`.

## Excluded, by design

Venue sourcing, catering, production, staffing on the day, badge and registration technology, selling or handing over attendee contact data, guest list visibility before guests accept, pitching from the floor, stage time beyond a welcome, guaranteed meetings, guaranteed pipeline.

## Demo and description rule

Nothing BLOCKED, IN BUILD or ROADMAP may be described as a thing a client can buy today, shown in a deck as finished, or narrated on a call as routine. If it must be mentioned, its status travels in the same sentence.
