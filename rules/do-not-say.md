# Do not say

The claim hygiene spine. Every claim is checked here before it ships. Left is forbidden or risky, right is the safe replacement. Every ban has a replacement.
last_reviewed: 2026-10-07

## Hard bans

| Do not say | Say instead |
|---|---|
| Any client count, attendee total, named client, logo, testimonial, quoted praise or case study | "We are new. No case study yet, and we are not going to dress that up." |
| Any conversion, meeting, lead, pipeline or revenue figure as ours, including "illustrative" | Describe the work. Offer the ROI calculator, where the client supplies every conversion input |
| We guarantee leads, meetings, pipeline, introductions or attendance | No guarantee until D4 and D10. "You know what this channel can deliver against your real list before you spend." |
| We bring you attendees from your ICP | Once D4 settles: a stated floor with a named remedy, in contract language. Until then, nothing |
| Our network, our community, our executives, our guest list | "Built per engagement from your named account list." The name describes the method, not an asset |
| A 70% reply rate, positive or otherwise | Nothing, until D3 reconstructs it from send records |
| Miro, or any client named | "A global collaboration software company commissioned and paid for two rooms." In private conversation only, and only once written permission is held for this use |
| I raised a $30M Series A after these trade shows | The permitted wording in `reference/founder-credentials.md`, gated on documentation |
| More than $1M in pipeline from hospital and pharma buyers | `{TBD}` until the artifact exists. Then the permitted wording only |
| The authority and experience of successful events | "Convened and delivered two commissioned rooms, and ran the exhibition programme at a health technology company." |
| Any price, fee, discount, tier, or The List, The Room, The Channel | "Written scope agreed before anything starts." No number until D2 and D10 |
| Inc., Ltd., registered, our business number, our office | Nothing. The founder by name is the sender. D1 |
| Any figure not in `rules/evidence-ledger.md`, or without its source | The ledger figure with its attribution, or `{TBD}` |
| A grade C figure in a proposal, scope, contract, deck or email | The grade B figure, or omit |
| Anything drawn from the healthcare guest list or the healthcare business | Nothing. A consent boundary, D6 |
| A format, event size, city or country in a client headline or on the website | "In person", "any format, any size" |
| Attendance or attendees as the product | Qualified conversations, with sales-qualified leads as the purpose |
| Pay-on-attendance as our differentiator against agencies | Use it only against trade shows, hosted-buyer operators and in-housing. Agencies match it |
| "Handed to your sellers with the context of what was actually said" | Frozen until D11. Draft-only: "qualified and handed over to your sellers" |
| To a guest: your details go to no one, including the host. Nothing follows | Frozen until D11. Still approved: "No attendee list is circulated, before or after." |
| Copy for paid ads, a cold email sequence, automated or high-volume outreach | Refuse and redirect: one founder-led post, or one researched individual approach |

## In speech, decks, the calculator and guest messages

- **Speech.** Say the Clutch source aloud whenever the 70% attendance benchmark is used. Never mention a reply rate in the same conversation. Asked "what do others usually see?", answer: "We do not know yet, and we will not guess. Put your own numbers in the calculator."
- **Decks.** `{TBD}` stays `{TBD}`. If a deck tool replaces one with a plausible figure, the figure is invented and comes out.
- **The calculator.** Opens with empty conversion inputs, never our defaults. The fee field stays empty until D2. The 30% close-rate button is grade C, agency-sourced: label it as an industry figure, not ours, or remove it. No result may display before the client has typed their own numbers. Never write "the programme is scoped to produce" a number of conversations until D4.
- **Guest messages.** Never a second message to a non-reply. Never a list of attendees. Never the host's sales language.

## Claims that need scoping

The counter-example stays in the file so the broad form cannot creep back.

- **No other channel can produce it.** Counter-example: a booth conversation is also a lead who met you in person. Safe form: "Every lead from this channel has already met you in person."
- **Access you cannot buy.** Counter-example: hosted-buyer operators such as GDS Group sell sponsors access to an audience. Safe form: "Access a sequence cannot reach."
- **Your highest-quality pipeline**, the landing page headline. Counter-example: no delivered engagement exists to compare quality. Safe form, proposed: "Turn the events you already fund into pipeline from people who have already met you." Founder to approve.
- **The channel did not get more competitive. It stopped working.** Counter-example: reply rates are 3.43%, not zero. Safe form: "Reply rates fell from 8.5% in 2019 to 3.43% in 2026."
- **Every channel that can be automated is being automated into worthlessness.** Counter-example: a published benchmark forecasts paid acquisition cost falling in 2027. Safe form: "Every channel that can be automated is being flooded." Then the dated trend.
- **It takes six weeks to get a named executive to commit.** No source. Safe form, as method: "We approach each person individually over a six week window."
- **AI content is eroding trust, so events are becoming necessary.** Counter-example: machine-written article share is reported level since mid-2024, and the trust surveys measure consumers. Safe form: "Trust in digital channels is measurably declining and the cost of reaching a buyer through them is rising. In-person contact is one of the channels that benefits."
- **In-person events have more trust and more conversion.** Counter-example: belief is not measurement. Safe form: "B2B organisations report events as their highest-confidence channel and, in similar numbers, report they cannot attribute the return."
- **It will keep getting harder and more expensive to get leads online.** Safe form: the observed trend with its dates, and stop.
- **The cost-per-lead table on the landing page.** Counter-example: the same 2026 source puts in-person events at $400 to $1,200 per lead, median $650, and the page omits that row. A prospect who reads the source sees a cherry-pick. Safe form: include the in-person row. The $800 to $2,500 per-meeting range is not split by channel in the source, so do not assign it to cold email and ABM separately.
- **A CISO fields around sixty cold approaches a week.** Grade C. Holds only on the landing page, attributed. Never in a proposal.
- **Only we sell the coverage read standalone, publish a composition report, or serve this segment.** A search-only pass cannot support "only". Safe form: describe what we do, with no comparison.
- **Pay-on-attendance is a category norm, and ConvergeX guarantees 50% meetings.** Grade C plus, third-party summaries. Confirm on the competitors' own pages before saying either on a call.

## Historical and reputational material

- **The founder's two commissioned rooms.** Real, and the same record includes three accepted guests who did not appear and a room cancelled four days out. Never call them successful. Describe the record and drop the adjective.
- **Aimedis.** The founder served as CMO during a period in which the company raised a $30M Series A. Causation is never implied. Gated on documentation.
- **Roster and room-centric positioning.** Superseded on 2026-09-22. Neither is mentioned publicly or presented as current.
- **The healthcare business.** Separate, with its own guests and references. Never borrowed, never referenced in this business's selling.
