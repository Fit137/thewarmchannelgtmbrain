## Rules that come with the numbers

1. **Figure 11 over figure 6** for anything about an invite-only executive event. They measure different populations. Figure 6 only for "payment predicts attendance".
2. **The arithmetic**: at 70%, twelve in the room needs about seventeen confirmations. Set any floor against confirmations, never invitations, and adjust for title mix.
3. **The 70% collision**: figure 11 and the founder's banned reply rate share a value. Never say both in one conversation.
4. **Banned outright**: the founder's 70% reply rate, D3, and every Warm Channel performance figure. None exists.
5. **Closing the grade gap**: the founder's own historical cost per lead and per meeting outranks every C plus line. One hour of work, `ops/decisions.md` D17.
