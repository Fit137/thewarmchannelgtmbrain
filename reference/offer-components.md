# Offer components and the demand driver

Load for: what the engagement is made of, what leads messaging, what is never priced separately, and the coverage read. Distils Run A Phase 1, the six-vector service and offer matrix rebuilt 2026-09-22.

## The components

The channel is sold as one thing. These are what it is made of and what the sales call configures. None is a website section or a price line.

| # | Component | What it is | Status |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Channel strategy | Which events, formats, accounts and calendar. The plan the client does not have | READY |
| 2 | Audience build | Finding the right individuals at the named accounts and getting them to commit | Research READY. Outreach BLOCKED, D5 |
| 3 | Programme running | Execution on the day, including the conversation design that makes it worth attending | READY |
| 4 | Post-event conversion | The follow-through that turns a conversation into a sales-qualified lead and hands it to the client's sellers | BLOCKED, D11 and D5 |
| 5 | Coverage read | A diagnostic: can this account list be assembled, and where are the gaps | READY |
| 6 | Measurement and attribution | Proving the channel's return in a form a finance function accepts | IN BUILD |

## What the tables say

- **Post-event conversion turns an events service into a channel.** Dropping it to save effort collapses the positioning back into the room business that was rejected. It is BLOCKED until D11 reconciles it with the guest promise, which makes D11 the most valuable decision in the brain.
- **Measurement is the sleeper.** Teams cannot prove event return in a form a CFO accepts and fall back on badge scans. Low effort once built, and it funds the second engagement.
- **Programme running is kept and never priced.** A commodity billed separately reads as padding.
- **The coverage read is a door, not a room.** Unique and barely wanted as a purchase, and the no-reference-needed way to open a conversation.

**The offer**: one bundled channel engagement, strategy, audience build, running and post-event conversion, wrapped in measurement, with the coverage read as the free or low-cost entry.

## The demand driver, the Channel Coverage Read

| | |
|---|---|
| What | The client sends its named target account list. It comes back with how many accounts have a reachable decision maker at the target title, which events those people already attend, and what a realistic composed audience from that list looks like |
| Immediate value | A number the client does not have. Most account lists have never been tested for whether their people can be assembled at all |
| Diagnosis | Separates two failures that feel identical: a coverage problem, too few reachable people on the list, and an access problem, the people exist and the client cannot reach them |
| Why it sells | Neither failure is fixed by a bigger booth or a nicer venue. Both are fixed by channel work the client is not set up to do |
| ICP fit | Exact. It needs a named account list, which is a qualifying attribute |
| Constraint | Says nothing about what The Warm Channel can deliver. No lead count, meeting count or named attendee |
