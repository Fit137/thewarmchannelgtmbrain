## How a research result enters the brain

1. It lands in `ops/signal-log.md` as COMPETITIVE, SEGMENT or PROOF with the verbatim finding and the source URL.
2. A figure is graded A, B or C by the rule in `rules/evidence-ledger.md` and added there before any copy uses it.
3. A competitor fact updates `reference/competitor-battlecards.md` and, if it touches a claim, `rules/do-not-say.md` first.
4. "No result" is recorded as absent or silent, never as "competitors have no weaknesses".
