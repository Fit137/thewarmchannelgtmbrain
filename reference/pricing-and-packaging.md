## Packaging

**Retired**: The List, The Room, The Channel. They described the room offer and its scopes, 2 to 3 weeks, 8 to 10 weeks and 12 months. Do not use the names in client copy.

**Current offer shape**, from Phase 1 rebuilt: one bundled channel engagement, strategy, audience build, running and post-event conversion, wrapped in measurement, with the coverage read as the free or low-cost entry. Programme running is never priced separately. Facilitation is absorbed.

**Principles that survive**:
- No free tier for the engagement. Payment predicts commitment (Pheedloop, B plus), and a free engagement attracts buyers who will not host.
- The coverage read may be free, because it qualifies and needs no delivery risk.
- Venue, catering, production and staffing are passed through: the client contracts and pays them directly.
- **Expansion levers**: a new segment or geography needs a new audience, a new title needs a new build, more events per year reuse relationships and carry the margin.
- **Rejected lever**: selling attendee contact data, introductions without consent, or the guest list. It converts the only compounding asset into a one-time payment.

## The contract term that is not a pricing detail

The Warm Channel retains the guest list, the relationships and the intelligence produced. A client that contracts to own the list is buying work for hire, priced materially higher or declined. The method is copyable in a weekend, the list is not.
