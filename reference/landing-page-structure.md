# Landing page structure

Load for: editing, building or reviewing the landing page. Distils `landing-page/PAGE-STRUCTURE.md` and `landing-page/COPY-NOTES.md`, both rebuilt 2026-09-22. The copy itself is `pages/landing-page.md`.

## Why the shape changed

Earlier versions were an essay: arguments in document order, a refusals table and a six-question FAQ. That reads as a position paper, which invites a reader to consider and come back later. The page now runs the `landing-page-copywriter` 14-section anatomy, a conversion sequence. The build must respect the emotional sequence rather than rearrange it for visual balance.

## The sequence

| # | Block | Job | Lead component |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0 | Masthead | Seriousness | `masthead` |
| 1 | Hero | Name the transformation, identify the reader, first call to action | display, `standfirst`, `icp-rule`, `action` |
| 2 | What your pipeline desperately needs | Three deeper needs | subheads, prose |
| 3 | What you're already doing right | Four validations, which earn the right to block 4 | subheads, short prose |
| 4 | But you're still | Five pains, each mirroring a validation | subheads, `hero-figure` x2, `filing-table` |
| 5 | Crescendo | One high-emotion transition. **No heading** | body prose |
| 6 | The solution | Positioning, "This isn't an events agency" | `pull-line` |
| 7 | The big promise | "Every lead has already met you in person", checkmark list, call to action | display, list, `action` |
| 8 | How it works in 3 steps | Plan, fill, convert | numbered `filing-table` |
| 9 | Beyond the 3 steps | Measurement | short prose |
| 10 | vs the alternatives | Comparison, cost table, calculator, call to action | two `filing-table`, `calculator-form`, `action` |
| 11 | Transform closing | Old way against new way | two-column `filing-table` |
| 12 | Founding client call to action | Scarcity, final call to action | `action` |
| 13 | Footer | Close | masthead styling |

**Blocks 3 and 4 are one mechanism and stay adjacent.** The pain lands only because the validation came first. Cutting block 3 for length turns block 4 into an accusation.

**Figures**: two hero figures, both in block 4: 40.8% (CEIR 2026, B) under badge scans, and roughly 1% (grade C) beneath it. The cold email decline is a three-row table, never a chart.

## Deliberately absent

No FAQ, which invites deliberation rather than action. No "what we refuse to do" block, which belongs in the scope document and the call. No second call to action of any kind, no scorecard offer, no newsletter, no pricing table. "Book a call", three times, identical wording. A second option is a way of not taking the first.

## The build must not

1. Reorder blocks 2 to 5.
2. Add an FAQ, an objections block or a refusals table.
3. Add a second call to action.
4. Name a format, an event size, a city or a country.
5. Use attendance or attendee as the product. The unit is a qualified conversation.
6. Add photography before consent tiers are settled.
7. Add a navigation bar. One page, one action.
8. Convert any table into a chart.
9. Introduce a second accent. Block 11's failure mark is `flag`, a status colour.
10. Soften the capitalised lines in block 5.

## Motion

Rules drawing in at 240ms, calculator figures counting at 400ms, the action darkening on hover, focus rings always visible. No parallax, reveals, 3D, glow, scroll-jacking or sticky elements. Respect reduced motion.

## Sourcing, still open

Most channel economics on the page are grade C plus. CEIR and Instantly are the only grade B. The founder chose this on 2026-09-20 to argue on lead generation economics, with every figure attributed on the page, which is the mitigation, not a fix. **One hour closes it**: the founder's own historical cost per lead and per meeting outranks every aggregator, D17. Every figure needs a footnote stating its source and grade in the Folio build.

## Open before the page goes live

The segment, D8, though the copy works for both leading candidates. The founding client terms, D13. Pricing never appears on the page, and when it exists it sits beside the cost table in block 10. The pre-ship claim fixes in `pages/landing-page.md`, D16. The conversion clauses held on D11.

## What the page does not claim

No Warm Channel conversion rate, lead count, meeting count, pipeline or revenue figure. The big promise is structural, true by the nature of the channel. The checkmark list is capability, not performance, and the moment one line acquires a number it becomes a claim that cannot be supported.
