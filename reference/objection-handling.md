# Objection handling

Load for: sales calls, replies to prospects, landing page edits, guest replies. Synthesized from the buying committee blockers, the competitive findings, the claim ledger and the guest journey. Each heading is the objection in the speaker's own words, then the honest answer. Where the answer is "not yet", it says not yet.

## Client objections

### "Who else have you done this for?"
Nobody yet, and say so first. "We are new. No case study yet, and we are not going to dress that up." Then the substitute: a free coverage read on their own list, which tells them something about their business before any money changes hands. Never reach for the founder's record as proof. If credentials help, use only the wording in `reference/founder-credentials.md`.

### "ConvergeX guarantees half the attendees book a meeting. Will you guarantee leads?"
No, not today. The guarantee is D4 and D10, and until they land nothing is promised. Then argue it as a position: an operator that guarantees meetings is pricing your pipeline into its fee. If a meeting count is what they must report upward, ConvergeX is a fair fit and say so. Never say their guarantee is false.

### "We already exhibit at the big shows."
Good, that is characteristic 2. Ask what last year's flagship produced in pipeline. Then statement 4: the same budget, redirected, or a programme built around the show they already attend. Never "trade shows do not work".

### "Our field marketing team can do this."
They can do it well, and they do not have six free weeks to approach named senior people individually. "Nobody underperformed. The arithmetic was never done." Show the confirmation arithmetic. Never imply the team failed. The person saying this may be the User, who can kill the deal silently.

### "Isn't this an events agency?"
"This isn't an events agency. This is a lead generation channel that happens to run in person." We do not source venues, cater or produce. We plan the channel, fill it with their people, and work the conversations afterwards (the last clause per D11).

### "What does it cost?"
Not yet set, D2. Say: "Written scope agreed before anything starts, and the first step, the coverage read, is free." Never quote a range, never reuse a calculator default.

### "What do your clients usually see?"
"We do not know yet, and we will not guess. Put your own deal size and your own close rate in the calculator. We will not put a number in either of those boxes." Never cite figure 32 as ours.

### "Will you contact our target accounts under our name?"
The approach names the host. Every approach is individual and researched, never a sequence, and the account list is theirs. Who is approached is agreed before anyone is contacted. The method is IN BUILD until D5.

### "Can we do one event to see if it works?"
Refuse or reprice. "The output comes from the work after the event, which runs for weeks. A single event gets judged on the day, and that is not a fair test of a channel." Offer the coverage read instead.

### "Who owns the list afterwards?"
The Warm Channel retains the guest relationships. They get the event, the host position, the reporting and the qualified conversations. A client that needs to own the list is buying work for hire, priced materially higher or declined. Settle it before scope.

### "You are one person."
True. It means founder delivery, not an account manager, and a hard ceiling of about two concurrent engagements. Say the ceiling out loud. It is a reason to book now, not a weakness to hide.

### "Can you invoice us? Are you a registered vendor?"
Not yet, D1. Never imply otherwise. If this comes up before D1 lands, it is the most urgent signal in the log.

### "We do not believe events work."
Characteristic 4 fails. Thank them and disqualify. The education cost exceeds the deal.

### "Can we present, or have a few minutes to pitch?"
A welcome, yes. A pitch from the floor, no. It turns guests into an audience and the next invitation into a decline. This is in the scope and the founder holds the line in the room, D7.

## Guest objections

Answer in guest vocabulary only.

### "How did you get my name?"
Answer exactly and briefly: the public source, and that the record is kept. The answer form is designed with counsel under D5. The compliance answer and the acceptance answer are the same answer.

### "Will I be pitched?"
"No deck, no panel, no stage time. Nobody speaks from the front, including the host."

### "Who else is coming?"
Roles and organisation types, honestly, including when the room is still thin. If it changes materially before the date, say so.

### "Will my details go to the host?"
FROZEN until D11. Do not promise "no" and do not imply "yes". Say: "Nothing about you goes anywhere without you deciding it." Use this only if D11 adopts consented continuation. Until then, escalate the question to the founder.

### "I am too busy."
Accept it without a second message. "Reply yes or no. Either is a fine answer." A non-reply is an answer.
