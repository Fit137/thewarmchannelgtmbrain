# Positioning verdict, the 2026-09-22 repositioning

Load for: the strategic fork that was settled, why, what it invalidated, and what it left open. Distils `POSITIONING.md`, the reconcile banners across both runs, and the commit history of the build repository.

## The fork

Three direction changes were paid for before this one held. The sequence, from the build history:

1. **2026-09-16 to 09-18. Composed attendance.** "The Warm Channel builds a room from a client's own named account list, runs the outreach that fills it, and is paid on the executives who arrive." Fintech vendors selling into Canadian banks, credit unions and insurers in the Greater Toronto Area. Three scopes: The List, The Room, The Channel. One promise, attendance, with a floor and a remedy. Pipeline refused in plain words.
2. **2026-09-20 to 09-22. A lead generation channel, not a room.** The room-centric positioning was rejected. Commits: "Reposition: a lead generation channel, not a room", "Rebuild on the lead generation channel positioning", "Rewrite the page as a conversion page, not a position paper".

## The verdict, DECIDED 2026-09-22

The Warm Channel is a done-for-you B2B lead generation channel built on in-person events. The format is a delivery detail and never appears in the headline. The ICP is defined by five characteristics, not by geography, and the page names no country or city. The four components are offer components for the sales call, not website sections. The website sells the outcome, and the call configures the components.

## Why

- Attendance alone undersold the whole and invited the buyer to compare on the cheapest step.
- A room-centric name and offer told every vendor planning a different format that they were not the customer.
- The buyer's real desire is sales-qualified leads, and the AI-driven decline of cold channels is the tailwind.
- The ICP was bound to a city, which is an operator convenience and means nothing to a buyer.

## The honest caveat the verdict carries

Cybersecurity scores highest on the criteria, healthcare is where the founder can already prove something, and most segment evidence is grade C. Picking an ICP on grade C evidence is a bigger bet than writing a page on it. The 2026-09-16 brief had rejected cybersecurity as saturated, and that evidence was not rebutted, only outweighed.
