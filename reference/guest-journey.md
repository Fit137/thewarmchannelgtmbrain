## Critical success factors

1. **Stage 2 is where most of this fails, silently.** An unsolicited approach to a senior person from an unregistered sender with no standing is a message that gets deleted. The partnership route skips this stage, which is why it is the only route open today.
2. **Stage 3 honesty, including when it hurts.** Disclosing a thin room loses acceptances now and keeps the cohort. Overstating it wins one evening and ends the relationship on arrival. It will feel like a small rounding at the time.
3. **Stage 7 is the whole business.** A cohort is guests who returned. Guests who came once are a cost that was paid.

## Blockers

1. **The consent position, D5.** Blocks stages 1 and 2 for every route except partnership. Infrastructure, like the tax number on the client side.
2. **No standing at stage 2.** No entity, no website, no prior event in the segment. Only a partner supplies standing.
3. **The cold start at stage 3.** The first event has no confirmed guests to describe. The demonstration event pays that cost once, deliberately, rather than on a client's engagement.
4. **The client at stage 6.** A host who pitches from the floor converts every guest at the table into someone who will not return, in one evening. Managed by contract and by being in the room, D7.
5. **The note afterwards at stage 7.** Its content depends on D11. Until then, send only the summary offer and the nomination ask.

## Accelerators

1. Nomination after the first event, the highest-converting route in the plan.
2. The association partnership, which skips stage 2.
3. The demonstration event, which runs stages 1 to 7 for a whole cohort at once with no client to disappoint.

## Timing and drop-out factors, operator-side, grade C plus

Named factors that move drop-out: persona (technical audiences worse), timing (the second half of the year, especially August, October and November), geography (US no-shows reported around ten times ANZ), pitch-style framing, vendor-led rather than peer-led speakers, and whether guests are reconfirmed at all. Directors and above attend at 62% against 56% for technical individual contributors (Clutch Events, B plus).
