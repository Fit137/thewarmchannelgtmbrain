## Critical success factors

1. **The coverage read at stage 3 is the hinge.** The only asset that gives value before payment, needs no track record, and produces a number about the buyer's own business. Every stage before it exists to earn it.
2. **A valid invoice at stage 4.** Infrastructure, not persuasion.
3. **The first engagement delivered as described.** It creates the proof set or confirms the buyer's prior that nobody can do this. There is no neutral outcome.
4. **The reference at stage 6.** Every `{TBD}` proof field becomes fillable here and nowhere earlier. The first client willing to be named is worth more than the second client's fee.

## Blockers, in order

1. **No registered business name and no tax number.** Accounts payable will not process the invoice. Every earlier stage can run perfectly and the funnel still fails at stage 4, after the goodwill is spent. Register before the first proposal goes out, D1.
2. **The competence reading.** The User slow-walks the list and the date. Sell capacity, bring them along, never go over them.
3. **No reference at stage 3.** The honest answer to "who else has done this" is nobody. The coverage read substitutes: a delivered artifact about their own list beats a case study about someone else's.
4. **A list too thin to compose from.** The read exists to catch it. Decline the engagement rather than accept it and hope.
5. **Post-event conversion frozen.** Stage 6 cannot be promised in its full form until D11.

## Accelerators

1. Lead with the coverage read. It compresses stages 2 to 4 into one conversation.
2. Run the demonstration event at the founder's cost. It collapses stages 1 to 6 for every observer and creates the first reference, cohort and funnel table at once.
3. Reconstruct the two commissioned rooms into an auditable funnel table, D3. It moves the proof set from empty to non-empty without delivering anything new.
4. Settle the promise, D4. It arms the strongest copy available.

## Capacity

One person runs every row, and the outreach window for one client overlaps selling to the next. **Two concurrent engagements is probably the ceiling** before either delivery or pipeline drops, and which drops will not be a deliberate choice. It is a pricing input and the first hiring trigger.
