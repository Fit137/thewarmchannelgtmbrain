# Founder credentials, the permitted wording

Load for: any time the founder's background might be mentioned, in conversation, a bio, a partnership brief or an about line. Distils `GTM-BRIEF.md` Section 5 and the claim audit.

## The rule

The founder's prior record is real, and it is a **credential, not a proof point**. A credential describes the person. A proof point describes a result The Warm Channel delivered, and there are none. A credential never goes in a proof field, a social-proof headline, a nurture email or a case-study slot, even in permitted wording.

Four of the claims below are gated on an artifact or a permission that does not exist yet. Gated means not used externally at all until the gate opens.

## Permitted wording

| Do not write | Write instead | Gate |
|---|---|---|
| "I raised a $30 million Series A from investors after these trade shows and exhibitions" | "Served as CMO at Aimedis during a period in which the company raised a $30M Series A, and led the exhibition and trade show programme that preceded it." | **Gated on documentation** before any external use. A Series A has many inputs and no investor would confirm the exhibitions caused it |
| "Resulted in more than $1 million in pipeline deals from hospital and pharma buyers" | `{TBD}` until the artifact exists. Then: "Ran the exhibition and trade show programme at Aimedis, to which the company's own reporting attributed pipeline in excess of $1M in qualified opportunities from hospital and pharma buyers." | **Gated on a CRM export or equivalent.** Pipeline is not revenue, and "resulted in" is sole causation |
| "A roughly 70% positive reply rate" | Nothing, anywhere | **Banned until reconstructed from send records, D3.** Positive reply is undefined, a polite refusal is a reply, the denominator was small and capped by venue capacity, and the founder's own records grade it as anecdote |
| "Miro commissioned and paid for both rooms" | "A global collaboration software company commissioned and paid for two rooms." | **Gated on written permission** for this use. Private conversation only even then. The permission contemplated was for a different business |
| "I have the authority and experience of successful events" | "Convened and delivered two commissioned rooms, and ran the exhibition programme at a health technology company." | Usable now. Drop "successful": the same record includes three accepted guests who did not appear and a room cancelled four days out |

## What is usable today, without a gate

"Convened and delivered two commissioned rooms, and ran the exhibition programme at a health technology company." That is the complete externally usable credential as of 2026-10-07.

## Where a credential may appear

- In live conversation, when asked about background.
- In a short about line on the website, if one is added. The landing page currently has none, which is deliberate.
- In a partnership brief, describing who will run the evening.

## Where it never appears

Proof fields. Social-proof headlines. The coverage scorecard and its emails. Testimonials. Any sentence that implies The Warm Channel delivered the result.

## The healthcare boundary

The exhibition programme was in health technology, and the founder holds the strongest guest relationships in healthcare through a separate business. Those relationships and that business's references are never used here, on consent grounds first and brand grounds second, D6. If healthcare becomes the segment under D8, this boundary still holds: the list is rebuilt, not ported.

## Gates to open, in order of value

1. D3, reconstruct the two commissioned rooms from send records into an auditable funnel table. An afternoon of work. It moves the proof set from empty to non-empty without delivering anything new.
2. Written permission to describe the collaboration software client, for this business.
3. Documentation for the Aimedis Series A period and role.
4. A CRM export, or the company's own report, for the Aimedis pipeline figure.
