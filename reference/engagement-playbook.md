# Engagement playbook, plan, fill, convert

Load for: scoping and delivering an engagement, and describing the method accurately. Synthesized from the landing page's three steps, Run A Phases 1, 5, 7 and 9, the evidence ledger and Run B. Nothing here has been delivered. Every step carries its status.

## Before anything starts

1. **Qualify** on the five characteristics in `reference/icp-client.md`. Decline below six-figure ACV, without a named list, or for a single test event.
2. **Coverage read**, READY. Their named list, one segment, back within a turnaround set in D13. Coverage or access, named plainly.
3. **Write the in-ICP definition** before outreach: title list, seniority floor, organisation list, named-account test. A vague definition means the supplier decides what counts on the night, which is the behaviour this business exists to be the opposite of.
4. **Scope**, BLOCKED, D1, D2, D4. What is delivered, what is excluded, the promise and remedy, the list ownership term, the fee basis. Half the page is what we do not supply, and that is a selling asset.

## Step 1. Plan, the channel around the account list, READY

- Coverage first, so the client knows which accounts can be reached before committing money.
- Format chosen from the answer: a small table, a large room, or a programme around a show they already exhibit at.
- Calendar built backwards from when buyers are still reachable, not from when the venue was free. Avoid the second-half drop-out peaks where possible: August, October and November (grade C plus).
- Title mix planned: directors and above attend at 62%, technical individual contributors at 56% (Clutch Events, B plus).
- **Result to describe**: the client knows what this channel can deliver against their real list before they spend.

## Step 2. Fill, with the people they named, outreach BLOCKED by D5

- Named individuals, not a job title and a hope.
- A researched, individual approach over a six week window. One approach per person per question. The host named.
- Confirmation arithmetic set before the date: at 70% attendance (Clutch Events), about seventeen confirmations for twelve in the room. Set any floor against confirmations, never invitations.
- Confirmations managed to the date. Reconfirm close to it, because most no-shows cancel ahead (grade C, internal).
- Disclose a thin room honestly to guests at every stage.
- Weekly confirmation report against target, IN BUILD.
- **Result to describe**: the people in the room are the people on the list.

## On the day, READY

- The client hosts and has contracted the venue, catering and production directly.
- Conversation design: one live question, set from what is live at the guests' organisations, named in the invitation. No deck, no panel, no stage time beyond a welcome.
- Nobody pitches from the floor, including the host. The founder attends the first three to hold that line, D7. Absorbed, never billed.
- Observers, at the demonstration event only, are disclosed in advance and do not pitch.
- No photograph of the table. No attendee list circulated.

## Step 3. Convert, every conversation after the date, BLOCKED by D11 and D5

As drafted on the landing page: every conversation worked in the weeks that follow, qualified, and handed to the client's sellers with context. This contradicts the guest promise, so until D11 lands:

- Do not describe the handover mechanism on any live surface.
- Do not pass any guest's details, words or context to a client without that guest's explicit choice.
- Recommended for D11: consented continuation. At or after the evening, each guest chooses whether to continue a conversation with the host. The conversion work is done with those who opt in, and the measure is qualified conversations from consenting guests.

## After the event

- Composition report: targeted against invited, accepted and arrived, by account and title, with coverage of the named list. IN BUILD. No fabricated sample data, ever.
- Summary to guests who want it, attributing nothing to a named person without permission. READY.
- One nomination ask per guest, never a list. READY.
- Funnel table with real counts and dates for every stage. It replaces anecdote, so fabricating it defeats its purpose.
- Measurement read-out in pipeline terms, IN BUILD.
- Reference request, only once the client is genuinely satisfied. No testimonial drafted in advance, for anyone, ever.

## Capacity and contract

Two concurrent engagements is the probable ceiling for one person. The Warm Channel retains the guest list, the relationships and the intelligence produced. The client receives the event, the host position, the reporting and the qualified conversations, in the form D11 settles.
