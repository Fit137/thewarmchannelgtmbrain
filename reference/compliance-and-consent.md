# Compliance and consent, CASL, PIPEDA and the record

Load for: any outreach plan, any guest or prospect message, the record system, and the counsel brief. Distils `handover/COUNSEL-BRIEF.md`, the decision sheet and Run B. This file is not legal advice. It records what is blocked and what counsel must answer.

## Why this is the binding constraint

The core activity of the business is unsolicited first contact with named senior people. Canadian anti-spam law and privacy law are the operating environment, not a footnote. Without a written position, two of three guest routes are closed, the demonstration event cannot be filled, and no client engagement can be delivered, because there are no guests. The constraint does not appear anywhere in the client-side plan, which is how it would get missed.

## The re-scope the 2026-09-22 positioning forces

The counsel brief was written for Canadian financial services executives and a Toronto room. It must be re-scoped before it is sent:

1. **Geography-free ICP.** Recipients may be outside Canada, so the opinion must cover which regime applies to a Canadian sender writing to recipients elsewhere, at minimum the United States and the United Kingdom or the EU, or the outreach must be scoped to Canada until it does.
2. **The segment, D8.** Under the cybersecurity default, guests are security and technology leaders at enterprises, not bank executives. Question 3 on conspicuous publication and relevance needs re-asking for that population.
3. **Client-side prospecting**, added as a question.
4. **Post-event conversion.** Does a consented continuation chosen at the event support follow-up by The Warm Channel, by the host, or both? What must the guest be told, and what record proves the choice?
5. **The standing list across clients**, the original question 8, now sharper because the conversion step passes context between parties.

## The questions counsel must answer in writing

From the 2026-09-18 brief, still valid: the defensible basis for a first unsolicited approach; whether a free, third-party-sponsored invitation is a commercial electronic message; what implied consent from a published business address covers and for how long; what it does not cover, meaning a follow-up after no reply, a second invitation months later, and an invitation for a different sponsoring client; PIPEDA obligations, the business contact information exemption and its limits, and holding a standing list across engagements; what records to keep and in what form; what the message must carry for an unincorporated sole proprietor and whether registration changes it; whether an association co-host's member relationship extends to The Warm Channel; realistic exposure at low volume and which errors attract enforcement; and confirmation that the healthcare list boundary is correctly read.

## The record, designed after D5, never in this repository

Every contact detail needs its source, the date obtained, the consent basis, every message sent, and any opt-out. The answer to "how did you get my name" is both the compliance answer and the answer to the guest's suspicion at journey stage 2, so getting it properly rather than minimally raises acceptance as well as safety. **No guest or prospect personal data goes into this brain.** Trackers here use IDs and counts.

## Sources of names, as planned in 2026-09

Published company directories, professional profiles, published speaker lists and conference programmes, association member listings where public, and inference of corporate email format. No purchased lists. No scraped personal addresses. Tens of contacts per event, each individually researched. A single approach, no sequence, and a reconfirmation to those who accept.

## Message requirements, pending D1 and D5

Sender identified, a mailing address, and a working unsubscribe on anything commercial. A sole proprietor with no registered name cannot yet supply all of this cleanly, which is one reason D1 comes first.

## The position to prefer

A conservative written position over a permissive verbal one, because the record system is designed around it before it is built rather than after.
