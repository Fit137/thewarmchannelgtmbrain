Load for: who we sell to, how to qualify or disqualify on a first call, and the segment choice. Distils `POSITIONING.md` and Run A Phase 4 (rebuilt 2026-09-22).

## Level 1, the definition, all five must hold

Defined by characteristics, not geography. The service is deliverable anywhere and **the page never names a country or a city.** Where the founder concentrates effort is an operating decision, not positioning.

| # | Characteristic | Why it qualifies | First-call check |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | High average contract value, six figures and up | Below it the arithmetic of a composed audience does not work at any fee | "What is your typical first-year contract value?" They will say it |
| 2 | Already spends materially on events | The budget line and the belief exist. We sell a better return from a channel they fund | "Which events did you exhibit at or sponsor in the last year?" |
| 3 | The buyer is structurally unreachable cold | If a sequence reaches them, the cheapest channel wins | "What is your outbound reply rate to that title now?" |
| 4 | Already distinguishes warm from cold | No education required | "What does a referred opportunity close at against a cold one?" |
| 5 | Current event spend converts badly | The cost of the problem, large because the spend is large | "What did last year's flagship produce in pipeline?" Not knowing is the answer |

**Characteristic 5 is the wedge.** 40.8% of exhibitor marketing budgets go to trade show exhibiting (CEIR 2026, grade B). Mid-market reports roughly 1% in-person conversion from trade show leads (grade C, landing page only).

## Qualifies

- A named target account list already exists.
- Someone owns events or field marketing and is measured on pipeline.
- Deal cycles long enough that a first conversation matters.
- Has run at least one event that disappointed them and can say why.

## Disqualifies

| Disqualifier | Say |
|---|---|
| ACV below six figures | "The arithmetic does not work at a fee that supports the delivery." |
| No named account list | "There is nothing to build from yet. Come back with the list." |
| Wants a booth, a venue or a production crew | "That is an events agency. We are not one." |
| Needs a guaranteed count of meetings or leads to report upward | "We will not promise that. An agency that will is the better fit." |
| Wants convincing that events work at all | Characteristic 4 fails. The education cost exceeds the deal |

## The tempting one, refuse or reprice

A vendor with high ACV and real budget who wants **one event, run once, to see if it works.** It fails no disqualifier and it will come with money. The channel's output comes from conversion work that runs for weeks, and a single event bought as a test gets judged on the day. Selling a channel and being measured on an afternoon is how this business gets a bad first reference.
