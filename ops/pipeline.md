# Client pipeline tracker

Prospects and engagements, by stage. **No personal data.** Use an ID, the company type or company name if the founder is comfortable with it, the segment, and the committee role of the contact. Names, emails and notes about individuals live in the contact record system designed under D5, never here.

Stages follow `reference/customer-journey-client.md`. Update after every call, and review in the weekly triage.

## Rules
- One researched approach per person. No row ever records a second approach to a non-reply.
- Every call produces at least one entry in `ops/signal-log.md`.
- Concurrent engagements in stages 5 and 6 never exceed two without a decision in `ops/decisions.md`.
