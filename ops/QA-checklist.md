# QA checklist, run before shipping any output

Go through every item against the draft. Every item is answerable yes or no by looking at the draft. If any fails, fix it before shipping. When returning work, state which blocks were run.

## 1. Writing
- [ ] No em dash and no en dash anywhere.
- [ ] None of the banned words: leverage, unlock, seamless, robust, streamline, cutting-edge, empower, or a variant.
- [ ] Paragraphs are one to three sentences.
- [ ] Commonwealth spelling: programme, organisation, colour.
- [ ] No stacked adjectives. Specific mechanism or number wherever an adjective was tempting.

## 2. Audience
- [ ] The draft names its one audience at the top of the working notes: client or guest.
- [ ] Guest draft: no word from the never-on-a-guest-surface table in `rules/two-vocabularies.md`.
- [ ] Guest draft: no client argument (reply rates, trust decline, badge scans, budget).
- [ ] Client draft: no copy lifted from a guest asset.
- [ ] Guest copy in a template or example sits between the guest-copy markers.

## 3. Channel
- [ ] This is a channel this go-to-market produces for. Not paid, not a sequence, not automation, not a follow-up to a non-reply, not an advertised invitation.
- [ ] Exactly one call to action. On the website it is "Book a call".

## 4. Claims
- [ ] No client count, attendee total, named client, logo, testimonial, quote or case study.
- [ ] No conversion, meeting, lead, pipeline or revenue figure presented as ours, illustrative or otherwise.
- [ ] No guarantee of leads, meetings, pipeline, introductions or attendance.
- [ ] No claim or hint of an existing network, community or guest list.
- [ ] No price, fee, discount, tier, or The List, The Room or The Channel.
- [ ] No "Inc.", "registered", business number or address.
- [ ] Every capability mentioned is READY in `rules/service-status.md`, or carries its status in the same sentence.
- [ ] Post-event conversion and handover wording stays draft-only until D11. No guest promise that details go to no one or that nothing follows.
- [ ] Every "only", "first", "best", "never", "no other" and "highest" checked against `rules/do-not-say.md`.
- [ ] Founder record, if used at all, is in the exact permitted wording and not in a proof position.
- [ ] Nothing from the healthcare guest list or the healthcare business.

## 5. Evidence
- [ ] Every number is in `rules/evidence-ledger.md`, with its source named in the copy or footnote.
- [ ] No grade C figure outside the landing page. None as a headline anywhere.
- [ ] Every category benchmark is described as the category, never as our performance.
- [ ] The Clutch 70% attendance figure and any reply rate do not appear together.
- [ ] Unknowns are written `{TBD}`.

## 6. Naming and strategy
- [ ] Vocabulary matches `rules/glossary.md`, including "The Warm Channel" with a capital T.
- [ ] No "composed attendance", no attendance as the product.
- [ ] Client draft: no format, event size, city or country in a headline or anywhere on the website.
- [ ] No framing that contradicts a decided verdict in `ops/decisions.md`, and no open decision presented as settled.
- [ ] Capacity, never competence. Nothing implies the reader's team did this badly.

## 7. Stage
- [ ] The draft matches the journey stage it is for, in `reference/customer-journey-client.md` or `reference/guest-journey.md`.

## 8. Medium-specific
- [ ] Spoken: every figure attributed out loud. Read aloud without stumbling.
- [ ] Deck: every `{TBD}` survived the tool. No figure was invented to fill a slot.
- [ ] Calculator: no default conversion input, no default fee, no result before the user types.
- [ ] Guest message: one approach only, question in the first line, the host named, the time cost stated.
- [ ] Landing page: no FAQ, no refusals block, no navigation, no second offer, blocks 2 to 5 in order.
- [ ] Visual: Folio tokens, square corners, no shadow, no photograph, one accent.

## 9. Voice
- [ ] Reads like the matching file in `examples/`.

## After shipping
- [ ] Long-form asset filed in `pages/`, or live surface logged in `ops/asset-index.md`.
- [ ] Any new reusable line proposed for `ops/copy-bank.md`.
- [ ] Anything the work taught you logged in `ops/signal-log.md`.
