# KPI dictionary

Every metric this business tracks, defined once so it is counted the same way every time. Targets are `{TBD}` until there is a first delivered engagement to set them against. None of these is a claim, and none may appear in client copy until it is real, counted and permissioned.

## Rules for every metric

- **Real counts only.** A metric exists to replace anecdote. An estimated or rounded-up number is worse than `{TBD}`.
- **Define the denominator in writing.** The 70% reply-rate problem was an undefined denominator.
- **Never compare our numbers to a category benchmark in client copy** until there are enough engagements for the comparison to mean something.
- Counts live in `snapshots/` and `COMMAND-CENTER.md`. Personal data never lives in this repository.

## Delivery, per engagement

| Metric | Definition | Formula |
|---|---|---|
| Named-account coverage | Named accounts with an in-ICP person present, over named accounts in scope | Accounts covered / accounts in scope |
| Confirmations against target | Confirmed guests against the target set from the arithmetic | Confirmed / target |
| Arrived against confirmed | Arrivals over confirmations at reconfirmation | Arrived / confirmed. Category benchmark 70%, Clutch Events, never a target |
| In-ICP arrivals against the promise | Arrivals meeting the written in-ICP definition, against the D4 number | In-ICP arrived / D4 promise |
| Qualified conversations | Defined by D9 and D11. From consenting guests only | Count |
| Weeks of outreach | Outreach window length, for capacity planning | Weeks |

## Guest side, the measures that compound

| Metric | Definition | Formula |
|---|---|---|
| Guest acceptance rate | Acceptances over approaches, per route | Accepted / approached |
| Acceptance after disclosure | Acceptances among those told who else was coming | Accepted / disclosed |
| **Repeat acceptance rate** | Guests who accepted a second invitation, over guests invited a second time | Second acceptances / second invitations |
| **Nominations per attending guest** | Names nominated, over guests who attended | Nominations / attendees |
| Founding cohort | Executives who accepted, arrived and would accept again. Target twenty: four to six anchors, three functions | Count against 20 |
| Anchors secured | VP and above accepted | Count against 4 to 6 |
| Guest trust incidents | Any guest complaint about being sold to, listed or chased | Count. Target zero, always |
