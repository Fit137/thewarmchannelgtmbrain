# The message that carries a show-week brief

Client copy, one named person, once. The brain's `templates/prospect-approach.md` governs it: this is its show-week variant. Under 100 words. The person's name and address live only in the founder's own mail client, never in the workspace or the brain.

```
Subject: [Show] week, from the outside

[First line, the reason: their presence, specifically. "Your team is at [Show] on [dates], with [stand or sponsorship]."]

I read the week from public pages: where your buyers will be, what already competes for their evenings, and what is still open. [One line on the most useful thing found, for example: "The evening before the floor opens has nothing announced against it."]

It is one page, and it is yours either way: [the private link]

If it is useful, thirty minutes on how you want to use the week.

[Founder's name]
[Sender identification as the law requires]
```

## Rules

- Send it when the show is 3 to 12 weeks away, and within a week of re-checking the open slots.
- The send build only: a private noindex link with a random path, or the two-page PDF. Never an HTML attachment, and for a security buyer attach nothing at all.
- No tracking of any kind. No follow-up to a non-reply: the brief stands alone. Take the link down after the show.
- Never a price, a result, a client, a guarantee, a reply rate, or anything that implies their team planned the week badly. The reframe, if one is needed, is capacity: "the week is crowded, and the open evenings go first."
