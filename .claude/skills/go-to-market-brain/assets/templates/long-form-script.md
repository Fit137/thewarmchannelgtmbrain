## Phase 2: Write (spoken)
Write for the ear. Short lines. Second person. Read aloud.

- HOOK (first {{15}} seconds): state the payoff or a real tension in the first sentence. No intro, no "welcome back."
- PROMISE: one line on what they will know or be able to do by the end.
- BODY: deliver each beat, each earning the next with an open loop or a "here is why that matters." Teach value before any mention of the product.
- PAYOFF: the clear resolution of the hook.
- CTA: one soft line.
