## Write
- **Title.** The query, plus a reason to click.
- **Opening.** Answer the query in the first paragraph. Do not make them scroll for it.
- **The naive approach and why it fails.** This is where trust is earned.
- **The right approach.** Concrete, step by step, genuinely useful without the product.
- **Where the product honestly helps.** One section, not the whole article.
- **Related questions.** The follow-up queries, answered.
- **Close.** One soft call to action.

## Guardrails
Teach first. The article must be useful to someone who never buys. No keyword stuffing. Every claim checked against `rules/do-not-say.md`. Comparison content stays honest about where the alternative is fine. QA before publishing.
