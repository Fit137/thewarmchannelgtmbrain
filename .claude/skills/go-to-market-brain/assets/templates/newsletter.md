# Template, newsletter send

- **Subject line.** Specific, not clever. What the reader gets.
- **Preview text.** Extends the subject, does not repeat it.
- **Opening.** One paragraph of value, no throat clearing.
- **Body.** One idea, delivered. Link to the anchor asset if there is one.
- **The takeaway.** What they can do differently after reading.
- **One call to action.** Soft, single, relevant to the stage.
