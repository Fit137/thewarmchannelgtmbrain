# Template, one-pager

Audience: [{{audience}}]. Purpose: [sales leave-behind / partner brief / internal enablement].

## Structure
- **Header.** What it is, in one line.
- **The problem.** Two sentences, in their language.
- **The three pillars.** One line each, from that audience's value proposition file.
- **Proof.** Only from the closed set in `ops/copy-bank.md`.
- **Status.** What is live today, stated plainly. Anything unshipped labeled.
- **Next step.** One.

## Guardrails
Fits on one page. One audience. No superlative that is not in `rules/do-not-say.md` as approved. QA before sending.
