# Template, landing page anatomy

Audience: [{{audience 1}} / {{audience 2}}]. Pick one. Never blend.
Funnel stage: [awareness / discovery / evaluation / decision]
Voice reference: `examples/{{example}}.md`

1. **Hero.** The single promise, in the audience's words. Subhead naming what it is and who it is for. One primary call to action, one secondary at most.
2. **The problem, stated as they experience it.** Their language, not ours. No product yet.
3. **The reframe.** The line from `ops/copy-bank.md` that changes how they see the problem.
4. **What it is.** One short paragraph. Plain, concrete, no adjective stacking.
5. **Pillar 1.** Headline, then the proof. Status flags in line on anything unshipped.
6. **Pillar 2.** Same shape.
7. **Pillar 3.** Same shape.
8. **How it works.** Three or four steps, concrete enough to picture.
9. **Objection block.** The two objections from `reference/objection-handling.md` that this stage actually raises.
10. **Proof.** Only from the closed proof-point set in `ops/copy-bank.md`.
11. **Comparison.** Honest, including where the alternative is fine. Nothing overclaimed.
12. **Trust.** Security, compliance, and status in the exact permitted phrasing.
13. **FAQ.** The questions sales actually gets, answered honestly, including "not yet."
14. **Close.** Restate the promise. One call to action, the same one as the hero.

## Guardrails
One audience. Every unshipped capability marked in line. Every superlative checked against `rules/do-not-say.md`. Proof points only from the closed set. Run `ops/QA-checklist.md` before shipping.
