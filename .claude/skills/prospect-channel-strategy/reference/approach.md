# Sharing the strategy

Load for: the message that carries the deck to the prospect, and what happens after it.

The strategy is a gift with a point of view. It is given once, to one person, with no strings and no sequence. It works because it is specific, generous and finished: the reader can act on it without us, and the last deck says so.

## Who receives it

One named person, the role that owns events or field marketing and is measured on pipeline, found from public sources. Their name and address live only in the founder's own mail client until the contact record system designed under D5 exists. The workspace and the brain hold the role, never the person.

Consent basis: the brain's `reference/compliance-and-consent.md`. A direct approach to a client prospect is open at low volume: one researched approach, sender identified, no sequence, and it is part of the re-scoped D5 brief to counsel.

## When

When the anchor moment is far enough ahead for its first table to be filled properly: at least eight weeks, so a first call and the six-week window both fit before it. Sent too late, the strategy is a post-mortem.

## The message

The brain's `templates/prospect-approach.md` governs it, in its prepared-strategy variant. Under 120 words. The reason first, their situation, never ours.

```
Subject: [Company]'s year in person, rebuilt around your named accounts

[First line, the reason: their situation, specifically. For example: your team is at [show] in [month], with a booth and a sponsorship the same quarter.]

[One line on the problem, in their own words, from phase 1's glossary.]

I put together an in-person channel strategy for [Company], from public information only: your event year mapped, and rebuilt around the accounts you sell to. It is yours, whether or not we ever work together. [The private link]

Every number about your business is left blank for you to fill in. If it is useful, thirty minutes to walk through it with your numbers.

[Founder's name]
[Sender identification as the law requires]
```

Never in the message: a price, a result, a client, a network, a guarantee, a reply rate, a second call to action, or anything that implies their team underperformed. The reframe, if one is needed, is capacity, never competence.

## Delivery

- The **send** build, never the present build. `deck_check.py` says which one a file is.
- A private, unlisted, noindex link, or the PDF. Never the HTML file as an attachment: mail filters treat attached HTML as phishing, and it reads that way to a security buyer.
- No tracking of any kind: no pixel, no shortener, no UTM parameter, no read receipt, no link that reports opens. Knowing whether they opened it is not worth what it costs in trust.
- The deck skill's `reference/delivery.md` covers hosting, expiry and the PDF.

## After

| What happens | What we do |
|---|---|
| They reply and book a call | The brain's `templates/discovery-call.md`. Bring the strategy, ask for their numbers, offer the full coverage read. An UPGRADE follows if they share |
| They reply with a question | Answer it, from `reference/objection-handling.md` in the brain, and offer the call once |
| They forward it internally | Nothing. It was theirs to forward |
| They decline | Thank them in one line. STOP. Log the reason if they gave one |
| No reply | Nothing, ever. A non-reply is an answer. STOP after the anchor moment passes, and take the link down |

## Never

- A follow-up, a "bumping this", a second approach to someone else at the same company on the same strategy.
- Publishing the deck, showing it to another prospect, or describing it publicly with the company identifiable.
- Reusing a sentence about one company in another company's strategy.
- Treating the strategy as proof of anything. It is a sample of the work, not a result.
