# Scoring rubrics

Load for: the gate (phase 0), the footprint map (phase 2), choosing committee roles (phase 3) and verifying questions (phase 6).

Every score in a strategy is a judgement made in public view: the deck says so. These anchors keep the judgement consistent between prospects and honest within one.

## 1. The gate

One evidence scale for every criterion:

| Score | Meaning | Register kind |
|---|---|---|
| 3 | Direct public evidence that it holds | P1 or P2 |
| 2 | Indirect evidence, or an inference with its basis written down | P3 |
| 1 | No evidence either way | none needed |
| 0 | Evidence that it fails | P1 or P2 |

### Criterion by criterion

| Criterion | 3 | 2 | 1 | 0 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **ACV, six figures and up** | Contact-sales pricing and case studies describing enterprise-wide rollouts, or a public figure | Enterprise logos and enterprise sales roles, without scale | Nothing either way | Published self-serve prices under five figures |
| **Event spend** | A booth, a sponsorship and an own event in twelve months | One of them | A single speaking slot or webinar series only | No in-person presence in twelve months |
| **Unreachable cold** | A C-level or head-of buyer in a regulated or high-security segment | A senior buyer in an unregulated segment | Mixed or unclear buyer | A buyer who starts with a free trial |
| **Warm and cold** | A customer advisory board, a reference programme, peer-reference language throughout | Some peer language, a user group | Nothing visible | A pure self-serve motion |
| **Converts badly** | A public statement that event return is hard to prove, or a job post to fix event attribution | Shrinking show presence year on year | Unknown, the normal score from outside | Not scored 0 from outside |
| **Owner and list** | A job post with "named accounts" or "target accounts" and "pipeline" | ABM mentioned in content or a hiring pattern | Nothing visible | A high-volume SMB motion |

### Verdict

- **NO-GO:** any disqualifier, or ACV or event spend at 0, or a total under 9.
- **GO:** 12 or more of 18, ACV and event spend at 2 or more, and no zero.
- **HOLD:** everything else. Name the one signal that would change it.

A HOLD is not a soft NO-GO. It is a watch: the prospect's next exhibitor listing, a new job post, a funding announcement. Record what would change it and where to look.

## 2. The footprint map

Two axes, -20 to +20, read to one decimal. Rank the moments first on each axis, then assign numbers that keep the rank. Round numbers hide a judgement that was not made.

### Density: new buyers in the room

| Range | Anchor |
|---|---|
| +15.0 to +20.0 | The segment's flagship, where the organiser's audience profile shows the prospect's buyer titles as the core audience, in numbers |
| +8.0 to +14.9 | A major conference or regional show with a strong share of buyer titles |
| +1.0 to +7.9 | A specialist or local event, partial fit: some buyers, many others |
| -1.0 to -7.9 | Mostly customers, partners or the wrong seniority: an own user conference, a partner summit |
| -8.0 to -20.0 | Almost no new buyers: internal, recruiting, investor or community events |

### Access: time across a table

| Range | Anchor |
|---|---|
| +15.0 to +20.0 | A hosted private dinner or roundtable, guests chosen, an evening of conversation |
| +8.0 to +14.9 | Their own conference: sessions, a customer advisory board, chosen time |
| +1.0 to +7.9 | Sponsored breakfast or lunch seats: some time, no choice of who sits down |
| -1.0 to -7.9 | A sponsorship tier, a logo, a keynote: attention without conversation |
| -8.0 to -20.0 | A booth on a show floor: whoever walks past, for minutes, measured in scans |

### The anchor

The anchor is the bottom-right moment (density positive, access negative) with the largest density minus access, weighted toward the larger spend. A booth at the flagship usually wins. Ties go to the moment furthest ahead in the calendar that still leaves a six-week outreach window.

No bottom-right moment means no wedge. Record it. The Lite profile or a HOLD are both honest answers.

### Consistency checks

- Two editions of the same series share a score and the second points at the first.
- A score moves only with a new register row. "It felt higher" is not a row.
- The chart's title claim is written after the scores, from where the moments fell, never before.

## 3. Committee roles

Choose two to four roles for the deck from the four to five found. Rank by:

| Factor | Question |
|---|---|
| Decides | Does this role sign, approve or veto? |
| Owns the problem | Is the evening's question this role's job? |
| Reachable at a table | Would this role give up an evening for a peer question? |
| Evidence | Is this role on the prospect's own public pages? |

A role with no evidence is not in the deck. A role that decides but never comes to an evening (often the CFO) is named in the notes, not on the slide.

## 4. Question liveness

| Signal | Counts as one |
|---|---|
| A session on it at this year's edition of a segment conference | Yes |
| A regulatory or standards change that forces it | Yes |
| A recurring incident class in the trade press this year | Yes |
| Practitioners discussing it in public communities, in their own words | Yes |
| An analyst note naming it as a shift | Yes |
| The prospect's own marketing about it | No. That is the host's agenda, not the guest's question |

Three independent signals verify a question. Two is a candidate. One is a guess, and is cut or carried with "would be verified before any invitation goes out".

## 5. Format fit

For each moment, the format that fits is the one that gives the highest access to the same buyers without asking them to change their plans:

| The moment | The format that usually fits |
|---|---|
| Flagship show, booth (bottom right) | Show-week private tables, one or two, away from the floor |
| Conference sponsorship (bottom right, near the axis) | One table the evening before the conference opens |
| Breakfast seats (top right, low access) | Upgrade: the same morning slot as your own table, chosen guests |
| Own conference (top left) | Keep for customers. Add an expansion table, apart from prospects |
| Partner roadshow (top left or bottom left) | Keep for partners. Reconsider only if it sits bottom left |
| No moment in a metro where Tier 1 accounts cluster | Metro tables, quarterly |
