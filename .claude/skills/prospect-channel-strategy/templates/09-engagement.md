# 09. How to run it, [Company]

Three ways to run the plan, one of which is us, said plainly. The plan is [Company]'s either way: that generosity is the point of a free strategy, and it is why the last deck is believed. What The Warm Channel would do is described in the present tense only where it is READY, and everything else carries its status in the same sentence.

time budget: 1 hour
inputs: the claim envelope, sections 1 and 3

## 1. Three ways to run it

| | In-house, with this plan | With The Warm Channel | A mix of both |
|---|---|---|---|
| Who plans | Your team, from this file | We do, with your team | We do |
| Who approaches guests | Your team, around other work | We do, once our privacy position is written | We do for Tier 1, on the same condition |
| Who follows through | Your sellers | Designed with you before anything starts | The same |
| What you keep | Everything | The host position and every account relationship | The same |

Adjust the rows to what [Company] actually has: an events team, an agency of record, a partner programme. The real decision is capacity: who carries six weeks of individual outreach per moment.

The highlighted column is the one we would recommend for [Company], with one line of why: [line].

## 2. What we would do, and what is not set up yet

Status words come from the claim envelope's service status table. A step that is not READY carries its status on its own line in the deck.

| Step | One line | Status, if not READY | Result to describe |
|---|---|---|---|
| Plan | The channel around your account list: which moments, which formats, which accounts. | | You know what the year can deliver against your real list before you spend |
| Fill | Individual approaches to the people you named, over six weeks per table. | Opens once our privacy position is written | Every invitation goes to someone on your list |
| Follow through | What happens after the evening, for the guests who choose to continue, designed with you. | Not offered until that design is settled | Agreed in writing before the first invitation |

The honesty line, verbatim: We are new. No case study yet, and we are not going to dress that up.

Never on this page: a price, a fee, a tier, a founding discount, a guaranteed count, a promise that the founder attends (D7 is open), a client, a result, or a line about a network of executives.
