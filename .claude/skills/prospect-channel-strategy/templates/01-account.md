# 01. Account intelligence, [Company]

Who [Company] is, how it sells and what matters to it this year, in its own words. This is an established business with a working go-to-market. The job is to understand it well enough that the strategy reads as if it was written by someone who has sat in its marketing meetings, without ever pretending to have done so.

time budget: 2 to 3 hours
sources: public only. Every row below cites the evidence register.
