## Guardrails
Never: a second message to a non-reply, a word from the never-on-a-guest-surface table, a list of who attended, a nominator's name used without asking them first, or a frozen line from `reference/value-proposition-guest.md`. Guest audience only, copy between the markers, checked by `scripts/lint_claims.py`. One approach. Question first. Time cost stated. Run `ops/QA-checklist.md` before sending.
