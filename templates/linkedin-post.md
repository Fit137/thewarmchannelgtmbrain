# Template, founder LinkedIn post

Audience: client, but assume guests read it too. Stage: awareness. Method: `reference/channel-engine.md`. Voice: `examples/linkedin-category-post.md`.

## Plan, before writing
- Archetype: [composition teardown / category / arithmetic / budget wedge / reply-rate trend / scope]
- The one takeaway, in one sentence:
- The figure, if any, and its row in `rules/evidence-ledger.md`:
- The one soft call to action, or a question. Never both:

## Write
- **Opening line.** The hook, standing alone in a feed. A dated number, a named public event, the arithmetic, or the reframe.
- **Body.** Two to five short paragraphs, one idea. The specific detail arrives by the third line.
- **The turn.** The reframe that makes it worth saving. Usually from `ops/copy-bank.md`.
- **Close.** One soft line, usually the coverage read: "Send one target account list and get the coverage read back." Or one question.

## Archetype notes
- **Teardown.** Public sources only. Who attended against who the host presumably wanted. No speculation about named individuals. No claim about the host's results. 300 to 500 words, one image.
- **Category.** Vendor-owned conferences attend at 19%, invite-only executive dinners at 70%, Clutch Events, 13,155 registrations across 191 events. Say it is a category benchmark.
- **Arithmetic.** Twelve seats needs about seventeen confirmations at the Clutch 70%. Why the gap between invited and arrived is the work. Capacity, never competence.
- **Wedge.** 40.8% of exhibitor marketing budgets, CEIR 2026. Same money, run as a channel.
- **Trend.** 8.5% in 2019, 5.0% in 2025, 3.43% in 2026, Instantly. State it and stop. Never "cold email stopped working".
- **Scope.** What we do not supply and why that protects the client.

## Guardrails
Client argument only, and nothing that describes guests, an evening or a named organisation as targets or inventory. No grade C figure. No figure without its source. No result, client, logo or testimonial. No format in a headline. No em or en dash, no banned words. Run `ops/QA-checklist.md` before posting.
