# Template, one researched approach to a prospect

Audience: client prospect, one named person. Stage: solution discovery. Channel: founder direct approach, low volume. Voice: `examples/prospect-approach.md`.

## Research, before writing a word
- The person, their title, and why they are the User or Manager in `reference/buying-committee.md`:
- Which ICP characteristics are visible from public sources (event spend, ACV band, segment):
- The specific trigger: a recent event they exhibited at or hosted, a hire, a new mandate:
- The one thing about their situation that makes this relevant to them and not to their competitor:
- Consent basis, per `reference/compliance-and-consent.md`:

If the trigger and the relevance line cannot be filled, do not send. Research more or drop the name.

## Write, under 120 words
1. **First line: the reason.** Their situation, specifically, not ours.
2. **One line on the problem**, in their language, ideally from `ops/copy-bank.md` customer language.
3. **One line on what we do.** "We run in-person as a lead generation channel, end to end: plan it around your account list, fill it with the people on it, and work the conversations afterwards." The last clause per D11.
4. **The offer.** The free coverage read on their named accounts. Nothing else.
5. **Sign-off.** The founder's name. Sender identified as the law requires.

## Variant, the prepared strategy

For a named priority prospect that passed the gate and has a checked deck, per `reference/lead-magnets.md` section 4. Under 120 words, the same five parts, with the offer replaced:

4. **The offer.** The strategy itself, already built: "I put together an in-person channel strategy for [Company], from public information only: your event year mapped, and rebuilt around the accounts you sell to. It is yours, whether or not we ever work together." Then the private link, and one line: thirty minutes with their numbers, if useful.

Send the send build only, as a private noindex link. Never an HTML attachment, and for a security buyer attach nothing at all. No tracking of any kind. Time it so the anchor moment is at least eight weeks away. Every number about their business stays blank for them to fill in.

## Never
- A second message if they do not reply. One approach, then stop.
- A sequence, a merge field doing the personalising, or a template sent to many.
- A claim about results, clients, or a network.
- A price.
- Implying their team failed.

## Guardrails
One person, one message, one call to action. Every claim checked against `rules/do-not-say.md`, every capability against `rules/service-status.md`. No em or en dash, no banned words. Run `ops/QA-checklist.md` before sending.
