# Template, one-page scope

Audience: client. Stage: purchase. Status: **BLOCKED** on D1 (entity, so it can be signed and invoiced), D2 (price), D4 (promise and remedy), D10 (value metric) and D11 (what conversion means). Draft the shape now. Do not send it.

## Structure

**[Client], channel engagement scope, [date]**

1. **What this engagement is for.** One sentence, the client's goal in their words.
2. **The named account list and the in-ICP definition.** Title list, seniority floor, organisation list, named-account test, agreed in writing before outreach starts. A vague definition means the supplier decides what counts on the night.
3. **What The Warm Channel does.** Channel strategy. Audience build. Programme running and conversation design. Post-event conversion, in the form D11 settles. Measurement and the composition report.
4. **What The Warm Channel does not do.** Venue sourcing, catering, production, staffing on the day, registration technology. Handing over attendee contact data without the guest's consent. Pitching from the floor, by anyone, including the host. A guaranteed number of meetings or pipeline. **Half the page is this list, and that is the selling asset**: it is the clearest sign this is not the thing the buyer has been burned by.
5. **What the client does.** Hosts. Contracts and pays the venue, catering and production directly. Supplies the list. Agrees the in-ICP definition. Holds no stage time beyond a welcome.
6. **The promise and the remedy.** `{TBD}`, D4. One number, one consequence, in contract language. Set against confirmations, never invitations. The 2026-09-18 recommendation was a remedy of a re-run rather than a credit, because a re-run costs founder time the business has and cash it does not.
7. **Timeline.** The outreach window opens a set number of weeks before the date. Weekly reporting against the confirmation target.
8. **Fee and basis.** `{TBD}`, D2 and D10. Pass-throughs stated.
9. **Ownership.** The Warm Channel retains the guest list, the relationships and the intelligence produced. The client receives the event, the host position, the reporting and the qualified conversations. A client that requires the list is buying work for hire, priced separately or declined.
10. **Data and consent.** How guest information is handled, per the D5 position. Nothing passes to the client without the guest's choice.
11. **Signatures.** Requires a registered entity and tax number, D1.

## Guardrails
Client audience. No guarantee beyond the D4 promise. No result, client name or testimonial. No grade C figure. The exclusions stay visible and are never trimmed for length. Counsel reviews the first one. Run `ops/QA-checklist.md` before sending.
