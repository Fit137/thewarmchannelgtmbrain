# Template, partnership brief, one page

Audience: partner, an association or a service provider already selling to the segment. Read from both sides: it is a client-side credibility route and the main guest-side supply route. Status: IN BUILD. The partner set follows D8 and is re-sourced in D15. The 2026-09-18 partners, CBA, CCUA, Advocis and Payments Canada, served the banking segment.

## Structure

**A small closed evening for your members, [partner], [date]**

1. **What it is.** One evening, a small closed table, one live question set from what is live at members' organisations. No stage time, no deck, no pitching from the floor.
2. **What your members get.** A real question, peers in their own function, permission to speak candidly, a written summary if they want one, and no attendee list circulated.
3. **What the partner keeps.** Its standing, its member relationship, and its say over the question.
4. **What The Warm Channel does.** Composition: who should be at the table and getting them there. Programme running. The summary.
5. **Who hosts.** Named. If a commercial host is involved, members are told who it is in the invitation.
6. **Consent.** Invitations go through the partner's own channel, under the partner's consent basis.
7. **List ownership.** Stated explicitly and agreed before the first event. A partner that keeps the list keeps the business, and The Warm Channel that keeps the partner's member list without consent breaks the partnership. Write which relationships each party holds afterwards.
8. **The ask.** One conversation about one evening.

## The membership question read, a gift
Offer to apply the coverage read method to the partner's own membership, as a read of which questions are live, with no individual named and no member contacted as a result.

## Never
- A claim of an existing guest network.
- A client name, a result, or a testimonial.
- Client vocabulary in anything a member might see.
- A referral fee promise before D1.

## Guardrails
No figure without its source. Member-facing lines in guest vocabulary only. Run `ops/QA-checklist.md` before sending.
