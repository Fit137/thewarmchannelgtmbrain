# Template, coverage read, one page

Audience: client prospect. Stage: evaluation, the hinge of the funnel. Method: `reference/lead-magnets.md`. Status: READY.

## Inputs
- The prospect's named target account list, as sent:
- The segment and the one target title agreed on the call:
- The in-ICP test used, written down:
- Public sources used: company directories, professional profiles, speaker lists, conference programmes:

## The page

**[Company], coverage read, [segment], [title], [date]**

1. **The question this answers.** Can the people on this list actually be assembled, and if not, why not?
2. **Coverage.** Of [n] named accounts, [n] have an identifiable decision maker at [title]. Table by account tier, counts only.
3. **Where they already go.** The events those people already attend, from public programmes and speaker lists. Counts and event names, never individuals.
4. **What a realistic audience looks like.** From this list, at this title, the size and seniority mix that is plausible, with the arithmetic shown: at the Clutch Events category benchmark of 70% attendance for invite-only executive dinners, [n] in the room needs about [n] confirmations.
5. **The diagnosis.** Coverage or access, named plainly. Coverage: the list does not contain enough reachable people at this title. Access: they exist and nothing in the current motion reaches them.
6. **What would change it.** Widen the list, change the title, change the format, or run the channel. Honest about which.
7. **Next step.** One. Usually a call to scope.

## Never on the page
A named individual. A prediction of who would accept. Anything about what The Warm Channel would deliver, any lead or meeting count, any price. Any figure without its source.

## Guardrails
Client audience only. Counts, not people. Every figure attributed and from `rules/evidence-ledger.md`. The arithmetic is category arithmetic, never our performance. Personal data never stored in this repository. Run `ops/QA-checklist.md` before sending.
