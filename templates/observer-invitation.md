# Template, observer invitation to the demonstration event

Audience: client prospect. Stage: evaluation. Channel: the demonstration event, run at the founder's own cost. Status: BLOCKED, D5, because the event's guests cannot be invited until the consent position is written.

## Purpose
Turn the cost of the demonstration event into a sales asset. A prospect watches an evening composed and delivered, and sees the method rather than hearing it described.

## Fields
- Prospect and why they qualify, against the five characteristics:
- The event's question, described in client terms for this message only:
- Date, place, start and end time:
- What they will see:
- What they get afterwards: the funnel table for the event, real counts, and a conversation about their own list:
- What is asked of them: that they observe and do not pitch:

## Write, under 150 words
1. **The reason.** Why they, specifically.
2. **What it is.** "I am running one evening at my own cost to show how the channel works, start to finish. I would like you to watch."
3. **What they will see.** The audience composed from a named list, the format held, nobody pitching.
4. **The condition.** "You will be introduced as an observer, the guests will know in advance, and observers do not pitch."
5. **The ask.** One reply.

## Rules
- Guests are told in advance that observers are present, in guest vocabulary, per `rules/two-vocabularies.md`.
- Observers never receive guest contact details.
- The invitation never describes guests as targets, leads or accounts. Say "senior people from [function] in [segment]".
- No claim of results from previous events. There are none.

## Guardrails
Client audience. One call to action. Nothing BLOCKED described as available. No em or en dash, no banned words. Run `ops/QA-checklist.md` before sending.
