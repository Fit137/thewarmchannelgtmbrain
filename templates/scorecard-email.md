# Template, coverage scorecard email sequence

Audience: client prospect who completed the coverage scorecard and gave an email, which is express consent. Stage: discovery into evaluation. Status: **BLOCKED, D1.** Every commercial email needs the sender's mailing address, so the sequence cannot go live before the entity exists. Scorecard spec: `reference/lead-magnets.md`.

## The four emails

| # | Day | Subject | Focus | One call to action |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 0 | Your coverage result, and the one number in it that matters | The result, the weaker of coverage or access named plainly, and the confirmation arithmetic for their planned event size. No selling | Reply with the date of the event you are planning |
| 2 | 2 | Twelve seats, seventeen confirmations | The arithmetic in full, with the Clutch Events benchmark attributed. Why the gap between invited and arrived is the whole job, and why it is a capacity problem, not a skill problem | Book the free coverage read on one segment |
| 3 | 5 | The two failures that look identical from the inside | Coverage against access, explained properly. What each costs, what fixes each | See what the coverage read produces |
| 4 | 8 | Last one from me | A final note. Offers the read once more and stops. Its content on outcome promises waits for D4 and D10 | Book the read, or reply no and the sequence ends |

## Each email
- Subject specific, not clever.
- One paragraph of value before anything else.
- One idea. One call to action.
- Unsubscribe and the mailing address in every send.

## Never in this sequence
A case study, a testimonial, a named client, an attendee total, a conversion figure, a price, or the founder's record in any form. The 70% reply rate, ever. Any email after a reply of no.

The 2026-09-18 version closed on "no meeting count, no pipeline figure, no attendee data". The repositioning retired the refusal of the outcome itself. The refusal of a guarantee may survive, depending on D4 and D10. Rewrite email 4 once they land.

## Guardrails
Client audience. Express consent only. No em or en dash, no banned words. Run `ops/QA-checklist.md` on every email before the sequence is switched on.
