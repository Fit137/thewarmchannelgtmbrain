# Template, discovery call guide

Audience: client prospect. Stage: discovery into evaluation. Use: the first call booked from the page or an approach. Load `reference/icp-client.md`, `reference/objection-handling.md` and `reference/competitor-battlecards.md` before the call.

## Before the call
- Company, segment, and the five characteristics as far as public sources show:
- Who is on the call and their committee role: User, Manager or Decision maker:
- The positioning statement to lead with, per `reference/positioning.md`:

## 1. Their situation, ten minutes, mostly listening
- "Which events did you exhibit at or sponsor in the last year?" Characteristic 2.
- "What did the biggest one produce in pipeline?" Characteristic 5. Not knowing is the answer.
- "What is your first-year contract value, typically?" Characteristic 1.
- "What reply rate does outbound get to that title now?" Characteristic 3.
- "How does a referred opportunity close against a cold one?" Characteristic 4.
- "Do you have a named target account list?" Without one there is nothing to build from.
- Write down their exact words. They go to `ops/signal-log.md` as LANGUAGE.

## 2. Qualify or disqualify, out loud
Disqualify kindly on: ACV under six figures, no named list, wants a booth or venue or crew, needs a guaranteed meeting or lead count, does not believe in events. Refuse or reprice a single test event.

## 3. The reframe
Capacity, never competence: "Nobody underperformed. The arithmetic was never done." The wedge: "The money is already being spent, and it is being spent badly."

## 4. What we do, and what we do not
Plan, fill, convert, in one breath. No venue, catering or production: they keep the host position. Status of each component per `rules/service-status.md`. Be plain about being new.

## 5. The next step, one
Offer the free coverage read on one segment of their named list. Agree the list format, the title, and when they will send it.

## Never on this call
A price or a range. A result, a client, a network. A close rate or "what others see". The founder's gated credentials. A guarantee. The Clutch 70% attendance figure and any reply rate together.

## After the call
Log objections and language in `ops/signal-log.md`. Update `ops/pipeline.md` with an ID, stage and next step, no personal data. Run `ops/QA-checklist.md` on any follow-up written.
