# Template, decision brief for the founder

Audience: internal, the founder. Use: whenever an open decision in `ops/decisions.md` needs to be put to the founder so it can be settled in one sitting. These are commitments about money, law and the founder's time. An agent prepares the brief and never makes the decision.

## Structure, one page

**D[n]. [Decision title]. [Date]**

1. **The decision, in one sentence.** What exactly has to be chosen.
2. **What it blocks.** Every asset, channel, decision and status flag that waits on it, from `rules/service-status.md` and `COMMAND-CENTER.md`.
3. **Options.** Two to four, each with:
   - What it means in practice.
   - What it unblocks.
   - What it costs: money, time, risk, and what it closes off.
   - What the sources say about it, with the file.
4. **The recommendation, and why.** One option. The reasoning in three sentences.
5. **What it does not change.** Explicitly.
6. **If decided today, the downstream.** Every file to update in amendment order: the `rules/` ledger, the master context section, the `ops/decisions.md` entry, the copy bank and templates, the portable control layer.
7. **Decision.** Left blank for the founder: the choice, the date, any conditions.

## Rules
- No recommendation on law without counsel. Say "counsel to confirm" where it applies.
- Never present a default as decided. A default is what the brain does until the founder chooses.
- One decision per brief. Bundled decisions get half-decided.

## Guardrails
Internal audience. Facts from the brain only, each with its file. No em or en dash, no banned words.
