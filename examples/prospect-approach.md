# Example, one researched approach to a prospect

Status: DRAFT, pending founder approval. The prospect is a composite, written to show the voice, not a real person or company. Bracketed details are what research fills.
Audience: client prospect, a field marketing manager, the User. Stage: solution discovery. Template: `templates/prospect-approach.md`.

---

Subject: [Event name] and your account list

Hi [first name],

You exhibited at [event name] in [month], and your team posted the booth twice that week. I am guessing the harder question came afterwards: how many of the people who stopped by were on your target account list.

I run in-person as a lead generation channel, end to end. We plan it around your account list, fill it with the people on it, and work the conversations afterwards.

The first step is free and needs nothing from you but the list: a coverage read that shows how many of your named accounts have an identifiable decision maker at the title you sell to, and where those people already spend their time.

Worth sending one segment over?

[Founder's name]
The Warm Channel

---

## Why this works

- **The first line is about them**, a specific, public, recent fact, not about us.
- **The problem is posed as a question they have already asked themselves**, so it validates before it diagnoses. Nobody is told they failed.
- **One line on what we do**, the approved plan, fill, convert sentence in the singular voice of a founder.
- **One offer**, the coverage read, which needs no track record and gives value before any money changes hands.
- **No claim of any kind.** No result, client, network or price.
- **It is sent once.** If there is no reply, there is no second message.

## Watch for
- The conversion clause, "work the conversations afterwards", is held for live use until D11. Until then, end the sentence at "fill it with the people on it".
- Sender identification and any required address follow the D5 position and D1.
