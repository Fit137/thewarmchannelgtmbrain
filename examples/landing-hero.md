## Changes from the 2026-09-22 source, and why

| Source | Example | Rule |
|---|---|---|
| "Into Your Highest-Quality Pipeline" | "Into Pipeline From People Who Have Already Met You" | "Highest-quality" is a comparative performance claim with no delivered engagement behind it. The replacement is the structural big promise, which needs none. Proposed safe form in `rules/do-not-say.md` |
| "working every conversation afterwards until your sellers have real opportunities" | "working the conversations afterwards" | "Until your sellers have real opportunities" reads as an outcome promise, D4. The mechanism stays, the promise goes |
| "That is the whole difference, and no other channel can produce it." | "That is the whole difference." | "No other channel" fails the counter-example drill: a booth conversation is also in person |
| Checkmark 3 and 4 | Unchanged, flagged | Line 3 is held on D11. Line 4 is IN BUILD. Both may stay in the draft, neither ships until its flag clears |

## Why this works

- The headline names a transformation the reader can picture and makes no claim that needs evidence.
- The standfirst does the qualifying with the "built for" line, which disqualifies on the first read.
- The big promise is true by the nature of the channel, so it survives the first sceptical reader.
- The checkmarks are capabilities, never performance. None carries a number.
