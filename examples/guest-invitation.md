## Why this works

- **The question is the first line**, because it is the only reason a senior person gives up an evening.
- **Where the question came from is said plainly**, and it is not the host.
- **Who else is coming is disclosed by role and organisation type, including how thin it is.** "[Number] have said yes so far", with the real number, is honest at stage 3, and it is the line that protects the cohort.
- **Where the question came from is a bracket, not a sentence.** An example that states a sourcing fact teaches an agent to invent one. Fill it only with what is literally true.
- **The time cost is exact**, start and end, because uncertainty is a quiet reason to decline.
- **The host is named and disarmed** in one approved sentence.
- **Only the refusal that survives D11 is used**: no attendee list. The frozen lines about details and follow-up are absent.
- **No client word anywhere.** No lead, account, pipeline, channel or access.
