## The Warm Channel go-to-market

The Warm Channel go-to-market lives in the `Fit137/thewarmchannelgtmbrain` repository, checked out beside this one as `../thewarmchannelgtmbrain`. It is the source of truth for positioning, messaging, claims and voice. Do not restate strategy from memory, or from older files in this repository, when the brain settles it.

Before any client-facing or guest-facing writing, in this order:

1. `../thewarmchannelgtmbrain/CLAUDE.md`, the router.
2. `../thewarmchannelgtmbrain/rules/two-vocabularies.md`
3. `../thewarmchannelgtmbrain/rules/service-status.md`
4. `../thewarmchannelgtmbrain/rules/do-not-say.md`
5. `../thewarmchannelgtmbrain/rules/evidence-ledger.md`
6. `../thewarmchannelgtmbrain/rules/writing-rules.md`

Pick the audience before writing. Never blend them:
- Client: a six-figure-ACV vendor that pays and hosts.
- Guest: a senior executive invited to an evening, who pays nothing. No client word reaches a guest.

Non-negotiables: the proof set is empty, so no client, count, testimonial or result. No guarantee, no price, no network claim. Post-event conversion and the guest promise are frozen until D11. No em or en dash. Banned words: leverage, unlock, seamless, robust, streamline, cutting-edge, empower.

For channel work, load `reference/channel-engine.md` in the brain. Run the brain's `ops/QA-checklist.md` against every draft. New market input goes to the brain's `ops/signal-log.md`, not into the copy. If the brain is not checked out, paste its `adapters/portable-control-layer.md` instead.
