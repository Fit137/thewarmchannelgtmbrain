# Run A, Phase 3. Positioning

Rebuilt 2026-09-22 against `POSITIONING.md`. The competitive set changed with
the product. When the offer was one composed room, the competitors were
executive event agencies. Now that the offer is a lead generation channel, the
comparison set is anyone a marketing leader would fund instead.

Four statements, because the channel is compared against four different things
depending on who is in the room.

## Statement 1. Against the executive event agencies

**For** an enterprise vendor whose buyers will not answer a cold approach,
**The Warm Channel** is a lead generation channel run end to end,
**unlike** executive event agencies, which are paid to produce an event and
hand back a list of who came,
**because** the channel's output is a sales-qualified lead, which is produced
after the event by work the category is documented as skipping.

**The proof line:** practitioner reporting is consistent that the vendors who
generate pipeline from events are the ones running targeted programmes before,
during and after, not the ones with the largest presence. Grade C, opinion.

## Statement 2. Against outbound and demand generation agencies

**For** a marketing leader watching reply rates fall quarter on quarter,
**The Warm Channel** is the one acquisition channel whose cost the buyer cannot
drive to zero,
**unlike** outbound agencies, paid media and SDR outsourcing, which all compete
on volume in channels that automation is actively degrading,
**because** an email costs nothing to ignore and an evening does not.

**The proof line:** cold email reply rates fell from 8.5% in 2019 to 3.43% in
2026 while AI tools pushed four to seven times the volume into the same
inboxes. Grades B and C plus, in `research/evidence-ledger.md`.

## Statement 3. Against doing it in-house

**For** a team that already exhibits and already knows the return is poor,
**The Warm Channel** is a channel operator who owns the outcome,
**unlike** hiring a field marketing manager, which buys capacity rather than a
channel and puts the hardest step, getting unreachable people to commit, on
someone with eleven other responsibilities,
**because** the work that produces the result is six weeks of individual
outreach and several weeks of follow-through, and nobody in-house has that
window free.

## Statement 4. Against the status quo, which is the real competitor

**For** a vendor spending the largest line in its marketing budget on events
and unable to say what it produced,
**The Warm Channel** is the same spend, redirected to produce people who matter
and reported in a form a finance function accepts,
**unlike** continuing to buy floor space and count badge scans,
**because** the problem is not that events do not work, it is that nobody is
running them as a channel.

**The proof line:** trade show exhibiting is reported at 40.8% of exhibitor
marketing budgets, the single largest channel line (CEIR 2026, grade B), while
teams commonly report being unable to prove event return in a form a CFO
accepts and falling back on badge scan counts (grade C).

## Which statement does the work, and against whom

| Who is in the room | Statement | Why |
|---|---|---|
| A CMO deciding where next year's budget goes | 4 | The budget already exists. This is a reallocation argument, not a new spend argument |
| A demand generation lead with a falling reply rate | 2 | The pain is current, measurable and theirs |
| A field marketing manager who ran a disappointing flagship | 1 | They have already bought an agency and know what came back |
| A finance function asking why the event line survives | 4, then 1 | Reallocation first, then who actually owns the outcome |
| A founder or CRO who does not believe in events | **None of them** | Characteristic 4 of the ICP fails. Disqualify and move on |

Statement 4 is the one that opens budget. Statement 2 is the one people repeat
afterwards. Statement 1 is the one that wins a competitive evaluation once a
category decision has been made.

## The claim that is not made anywhere above

None of these statements says The Warm Channel produces more leads, better
leads, or leads at a rate anybody can name. Every one of them is a statement
about **where the work happens** and **who owns the outcome**, which are
verifiable facts about the service, rather than a performance claim that would
require a delivered engagement to support.

That restraint is deliberate and it is load bearing. `GTM-BRIEF.md` Section 5
forbids a conversion, meeting, pipeline or revenue figure, and the honest
version of this positioning does not need one: the argument is that the
category skips a step, and that is checkable without any Warm Channel data.

The first delivered engagement is what converts these from arguments into
claims. Until then they stay arguments, and they are stronger as arguments than
as numbers nobody can audit.
