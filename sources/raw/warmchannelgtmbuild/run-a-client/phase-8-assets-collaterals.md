The skill hardcodes three campaigns: LinkedIn outreach via HeyReach, cold email via Instantly, and LinkedIn ads. `SKILL-ADAPTATION.md` replaces all three and says to name the reasoning rather than just the substitution, because whoever runs this may disagree and should be able to argue with it.

## Why the skill's three channels are wrong here

| Skill channel | Why it is replaced |
|---|---|
| LinkedIn outreach via HeyReach, high volume | The business argues that cold digital outreach is decaying and expensive. Running it at volume is a weak bet on the numbers and a credibility problem the moment a prospect notices the seller using the channel they were told is failing |
| Cold email via Instantly | The same, plus the operating environment. Canadian anti-spam law governs the core activity of this business, and open decision 5 is unsettled. Volume sequencing before counsel has given a written position is an unpriced risk |
| LinkedIn ads | Paid against an unproven offer with no conversion data buys expensive evidence. A pre-launch business with `{TBD}` burn and `{TBD}` runway cannot afford to buy evidence it could get by delivering one room |

**The distinction that matters, and it is not "no outreach."** This business is outreach. The replacement is not volume outreach against its own prospects. Low-volume, high-research direct approach stays, because it is both the honest channel and the method being sold.

## The three channels

| Channel | Why |
|---|---|
| **1. Founder-led organic and low-volume direct approach** | The thesis and the channel agree. Small numbers of well-researched approaches is the method being sold, so the acquisition motion demonstrates the product |
| **2. The demonstration room**, run at the founder's own cost, with prospective clients invited to observe | The product as its own top-of-funnel asset. It produces the first reference, the first guest cohort and the first auditable funnel table in one exercise, and converts the pre-launch problem into an asset rather than waiting it out |
| **3. Partnerships** with slot 6 associations and with service providers already selling to fintech vendors | Borrowed credibility while there is none of its own, and a second route to guest-side supply |

| Any proof point, testimonial or reference asset | A delivered room |

## What this plan does not include, and why

No paid advertising. No sequenced cold email at volume. No content calendar built on posting frequency. Each was considered and each fails the same test: it buys reach the business cannot yet convert, using money it has not counted, on an offer that has never been delivered. The demonstration room buys the same evidence and keeps the asset afterwards.
