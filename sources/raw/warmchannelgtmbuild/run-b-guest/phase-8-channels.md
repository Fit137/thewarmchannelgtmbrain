# Run B, Phase 8. Channels

> **RECONCILE.** The guest side mostly survives the 2026-09-22
> repositioning, because a guest attends a convening whatever the client
> bought. Two things are stale and they are not cosmetic. The guest ICP here is
> a named executive at a Canadian bank, credit union or insurer, which followed
> from the old client ICP and no longer does. And the format is fixed at a
> table of twelve, which the new positioning explicitly refuses to name. The
> two-vocabulary rule, the consent boundaries and the attendance arithmetic are
> unaffected and remain correct.

The skill hardcodes LinkedIn outreach at volume, cold email sequencing and LinkedIn ads. For the guest side all three are replaced, and the replacement is narrower than Run A's.

**No advertising. No sequenced email. Ever, on this side.** An advertised invitation is not an invitation, it is a registration page, and it selects for people with free evenings rather than for the executives the room needs. A sequenced follow-up to a senior person who did not reply is the behaviour this business exists to be the opposite of, and it is the behaviour most likely to end a relationship before it starts.

## The three channels

### 1. Nomination from confirmed guests

Highest conversion of any route in either run, and it does not exist until one room has happened.

| Stage | Asset | Description | Format | Specs and QA | Inputs | Timeframe | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| TOFU | The nomination ask | After a room, each guest is asked for one peer who should have been there | One line, in the note afterwards | One name. Never a list. Asking for a list turns a guest into a data source, which is the thing they were promised would not happen | A delivered room | Same week | The wording matters. "Who should have been in that room" gets a name. "Who else would find this useful" gets nothing |
| TOFU | The nominated approach | The approach to the nominated person, naming the nominator with permission | Direct message or email | **Permission to use the nominator's name is asked explicitly and in advance.** Using it without asking spends two relationships at once | A nomination, and permission | Per nomination | Converts far better than a cold approach because it answers stage 2 of the journey, who is this and how did they get my name |
| MOFU | The summary, sent | The written account of the evening, sent to attendees who wanted it | Document | No attribution of anything said to a named person, unless they asked for it | The room | Two weeks after | The cheapest reason to accept again, and the artifact a nominator forwards |

### 2. Founder direct approach, low volume and high research

The core method, and the same method sold to clients on the Run A side.

| Stage | Asset | Description | Format | Specs and QA | Inputs | Timeframe | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| TOFU | The researched approach | One named executive, researched properly, approached once, with the question in the first line | Direct message or email, short | **Once. No sequence, no follow-up, no second attempt on the same question.** A non-reply is an answer | Consent position, open decision 5 | Ongoing | Volume is deliberately low. Twenty well-researched approaches beats four hundred sent |
| TOFU | The public question | The question posted publicly, without an invitation attached, to see whether it lands | Post | No invitation in the post. No capture form. It is a test of the question, not a channel | The question | Per question | Cheap way to test whether a question is live before spending approaches on it |
| MOFU | The disclosure of the room | Roles and institution types of who has accepted, sent to those considering | Message | Honest, including when the room is thin. Updated if it changes materially | Confirmed acceptances | Per room | Journey stage 3. The discipline is to disclose a thin room rather than imply a full one |
| BOFU | The invitation | The actual invitation, with time, place, duration and what is not asked | Message | Carries the three refusals: no list circulated, no details passed, no follow-up | The above | Per room | Drills 1, 2, 4, 5 and one line of 7 |

**The consequence, stated plainly.** Two of the three guest-side channels are closed until the consent question is answered, and the third is the slowest. This is the binding constraint on the entire business, because Run A cannot be delivered without guests, and it is not visible anywhere in Run A's own documents.

## Volume, and why it stays low on purpose

The Run A argument is that cold digital outreach is decaying and expensive, and that small numbers of well-researched approaches outperform volume. Run B is where that argument is tested on the business's own behaviour.

If the guest side is built with volume sequencing, the argument is false in the seller's own hands and a prospect will eventually notice. If it is built with low-volume research and it works, the acquisition motion is the case study, and it is the only case study available.
