# How The Warm Channel Works In 3 Steps

## 1. **Plan**, the channel around your account list

**Stop buying floor space. Start building a channel.**

We take the companies you want as customers and design the channel that reaches them. Which formats, which moments, which accounts, what calendar.

- **Coverage read first**, so you know which of your accounts can actually be reached before anyone commits money
- **Format chosen from the answer**, whether that is a small table, a large room, or a programme built around a show you already exhibit at
- **Calendar built backwards** from when your buyers are still reachable, not from when the venue was free

**Result:** You know what this channel can deliver against your real list, before you spend.

## 2. **Fill**, it with the people you named

**Stop hoping the right people wander by. Start choosing them.**

We find the actual decision maker at each account and approach them individually over several weeks. This is the part that does not get done in-house, and it is the part that decides everything.

- **Named individuals**, not a job title and a hope
- **Researched, individual approaches** over a six week window, because that is what works on someone rejecting sixty cold approaches a week
- **Confirmation managed** all the way to the date, not counted on the day

**Result:** The people in the room are the people on your list.

## 3. **Convert**, every conversation after the date

**Stop handing sales a badge list. Start handing them opportunities.**

The event is the middle of this channel, not the end. Practitioner reporting is consistent on this: the vendors who generate pipeline from events are the ones running programmes before, during **and after**.

- **Every conversation worked** in the weeks that follow, while it is still warm
- **Qualified and handed over** to your sellers with the context of what was actually said
- **Nothing left to cool** because the follow-through was nobody's job

**Result:** Your sellers receive opportunities, not contacts.
